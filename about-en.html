About

Proven experience in project development

AREX Mining S.A. is an Argentine mining services company. Our team develops copper and lithium projects for their owners, from exploration through to production.

Who we are

Mining project developers

AREX Mining brings together professionals with long track records in exploring, studying, permitting and building mining projects in Argentina and internationally.

We work with project owners to bring their project to market.

Track record

We have done this before

Our team has experience working across some of the largest mining and exploration projects in Africa, Europe and Oceania.

In Argentina, members of our team helped develop Lithium Americas' Cauchari project and Neo Lithium's Tres Quebradas (3Q) project. We are now bringing that experience to a new portfolio of copper and lithium projects.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To develop mining projects in harmony with the environment, respecting local communities and contributing meaningfully to the country's development.

Vision

To be recognised as a responsible developer of mining projects.

Values

Transparency, honesty and trust are the ethical foundations of how we negotiate. Integrity defines our spirit, and we act with responsibility and commitment. We believe in sustainable results and continuous improvement.
