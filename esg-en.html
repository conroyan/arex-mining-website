ESG

Responsible mining development

We develop mining projects in harmony with the environment, respecting local communities and contributing meaningfully to the country's development.

Our commitments

How we develop every project

Environment

We build environmental assessment and monitoring into each project from its earliest stages, with systems for risk management, compliance and continuous improvement.

Water

We treat water management as central to each project's design, safeguarding communities' access to their water sources.

Communities

We maintain clear and continuous communication with the communities near our projects, from exploration through to operation.

Local jobs and suppliers

We prioritise hiring people and suppliers from the provinces where we work.

Transparency

We act with transparency, honesty and trust in our dealings with authorities, communities, partners and investors.

Governance

We apply sound corporate governance practices and are accountable to the owners of the projects we develop.

Working together

With authorities and communities

We work alongside provincial authorities, technical specialists and local communities to ensure transparent decision-making, effective environmental management and protection of the surrounding environment throughout each project's life.
