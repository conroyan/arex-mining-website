The project

One property, two tracks in parallel

San Romeleo is a mining property in the department of Malargüe, in the south of Mendoza province. Originally promoted by LABSA S.A., the project is now operated by AREX Mining S.A. and developed for AREX Copper.

The project is advancing along two administrative tracks over the same mining asset, each with its own environmental assessment, clearly defined scope and no overlapping permits.

Tracks

How San Romeleo is advancing

Productive reactivation

An individual file for the small-scale mining of an oxidised copper body and the operation of a processing plant to produce copper sulphate.

Exploration

The property is part of the third group of the Malargüe Western Mining District (MDMO III), currently under environmental assessment. Planned work includes surface geological mapping, sampling, geophysical studies and diamond drilling, along with low-impact auxiliary works.

Our role

What AREX Mining brings

  • Permitting and environmental assessments
  • Exploration planning and execution
  • Engineering and development of the processing plant
  • Engagement with Malargüe authorities and communities
