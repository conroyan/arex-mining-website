Welcome to AREX Mining

Mineral exploration and mining project development

We are leaders in mineral exploration and mining project development. Our highly skilled team stands out for its dedication and integrity, and innovation and research allow us to break new ground in the industry.

We are dedicated to turning challenges into opportunities and bringing innovative mining projects to life, always committed to sustainability and environmental responsibility.

Today our team is developing the San Romeleo copper project in Malargüe, Mendoza, for AREX Copper, and the Tres Cruces Project in Catamarca for 3C Resources.

Services

One team, from exploration to production

We bring together every discipline needed to take a project from discovery to operation.

Exploration and geology

Surface geological mapping, sampling, geophysics, diamond drilling and hydrogeology to identify and define mineral resources.

Studies and engineering

Management of scoping, pre-feasibility and feasibility studies, and coordination of engineering with specialist consultancies.

Processing and technology

Laboratory testwork, piloting and process development to define the processing route for each deposit.
