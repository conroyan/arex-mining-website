The project

Battery-grade lithium on the Puna

The Tres Cruces Project is a large-scale, battery-grade lithium deposit in the Catamarca Province of north-west Argentina, approximately 30 km from the Chilean border and 154 km from Fiambalá.

It is centred on the Tres Cruces volcano and the alluvial cone beside the Tres Quebradas salar. The project is owned by 3C Resources and operated by its subsidiary Minera Cordillera, which owns or holds rights over approximately 30,000 hectares in the basin.

Status

Towards production

  1. 2025

    Geophysics confirms the hyper-saline aquifer and the first four drill holes are completed.

  2. 2026

    Ten drill holes completed. Pre-feasibility study finished and feasibility study under way.

  3. 2028 – 2029

    Targeted start of production, initially 20,000 tonnes per annum with a pathway to 40,000.
